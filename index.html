Restricted

Thanks to their Heavenly Restriction, the user's body has seen vast improvements that make them a fighter on par with sorcerers and curses alike. However, it also lacks any trace of cursed energy at all, which changes the odds against the user as:

  • They can go through borders of any and all domain expansions at will, being able to enter whenever they wish.
  • They are immune to cursed techniques that rely on detecting or disrupting cursed energy, making them unpredictable in combat.
  • Their physical strength and speed surpass normal human limits, allowing them to overwhelm opponents with sheer force.
  • Weapons and tools become their primary means of battle, turning every fight into a test of skill and adaptability.
